06 · Likely Gaps

What the brief doesn't mention yet

01
Offline / unsynced states

An explicit "unsynced" indicator distinct from a normal saved state.

02
Consent & e-signature capture

ABDM consent artefacts need their own pattern, with audit trail.

03
Skeleton states for FHIR calls

Medplum/CARE round-trips need loading skeletons, not blank flashes.

04
Empty states

"No allergies recorded" must read differently from "still loading."

05
Print / PDF stylesheet

Sidebar, gradient chrome, and cards should never print.

06
Gloved-touch density mode

Bedside tablet use needs larger hit areas than desktop.

07
Barcode / QR scan feedback

Medication administration (5 Rights) needs a dedicated confirm state.

08
Audit trail affordance

Inline "who changed this" on any edited clinical value.

09
Multilingual / Indian formats

Hindi/regional language, DD-MM-YYYY, ₹ as first-class, not a locale afterthought.

10
Real dark mode

A true night-shift variant, not an automatic invert of these tokens.

11
Keyboard-first workflows

Documented shortcuts and focus order for high-volume charting.

12
Tenant-brandable tokens

Split fixed system tokens (signal colors) from tenant tokens (logo, one accent) for multitenancy.

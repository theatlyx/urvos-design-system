Rules — do
DOUse Plex Mono with tabular figures for any number in a table column, so digits never visually drift out of alignment.
DOReserve Fraunces for moments that need warmth — hero, AI panel, a handful of card headlines. Never body text.
DOKeep line-height ≥1.5 for any paragraph a clinician reads under time pressure.
Rules — don't
DON'TSet a dosage, lab value, or vital sign in Inter — non-tabular digits can misalign in a way that reads as a different number at a glance.
DON'TUse more than one weight of Fraunces on a single screen — it starts to look decorative rather than intentional.
DON'TGo below 11px for anything a clinician needs to act on — micro-text is for metadata only, never for a clinical value.

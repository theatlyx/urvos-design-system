Nursing, Prescriptions, Patients & Reports

MAR and the vitals flowsheet are arguably the two most-used screens in any real EMR and were the biggest gap in the system. Formal prescription and lab report documents are the artifacts that leave the building — printed, faxed, or handed to a patient — so they get their own careful treatment.
